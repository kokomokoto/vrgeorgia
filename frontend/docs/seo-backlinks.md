# Vhome — backlink / mention strategy (ops checklist)

Goal: earn relevant links and citations that point to `https://vhome.ge` (apex).

## Priority targets
1. Google Business Profile + Maps (NAP consistency: Vhome, vhome.ge)
2. Georgian real-estate directories and classified aggregators (only where editorial/partner links are allowed)
3. Agent / brokerage partner pages linking to their Vhome profile (`/agents/{id}`)
4. Architecture / interior studios featured under `/services` — reciprocal “listed on Vhome” mentions
5. Local press / blogs: market explainers, VR tour features, city guides (Tbilisi, Batumi, etc.)
6. Social profiles (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn) with website = `https://vhome.ge`

## Content that attracts links
- Unique listing pages with VR/3D tours (`/property/{id}`)
- Map search landing (`/map`)
- FAQ + About as citation sources (`/faq`, `/about`)
- `llms.txt` + sitemap for discovery (`/llms.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`)

## Hygiene
- Always share canonical URLs (`https://vhome.ge/...`, never `www` or `vrgeorgia.ge`)
- Prefer linking to specific listings/agents over homepage-only mentions
- Disavow only spammy paid directories if Search Console shows toxic patterns

## Tracking
- Search Console → Links report monthly
- Keep a simple sheet: source URL, target URL, date, status
